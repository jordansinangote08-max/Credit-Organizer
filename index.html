Ways to avoid borrowing
  • Never pay late. A late payment hurts your credit score more than anything else.
  • Call the bank before the due date if a bill won't fit. Ask about a payment arrangement while you're still on time.
  • Cut food delivery, coffee and subscriptions until the gap is gone. ₱500–1,000 saved is ₱500–1,000 you don't borrow.
  • No card swipes or "pay later" until your installments are done.
